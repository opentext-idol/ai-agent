The 'Appending' context allows access to any part of the FlowFileDocument for reading, and for appending new or replacement parts. Parts that are not explicitly replaced remain in the document.

An 'Appending' context can be started with one of the following methods:

  • FlowFileDocument.append(...)
  • for parts in FlowFileDocument.partsAppend()
  • with FlowFileDocument.appending() as action
