Content text parts of a FlowFileDocument are segments of the content of a FlowFile containing UTF-8 character data. The content text of a single part may be split into pages. The 'Form Feed' or 'New Page' control character 12 is used to separate pages within a single part.

The following methods can be used to read and write content text parts:

  • for page in FlowFileDocument.getContentPages()
  • FlowFileDocument.setContentPages(pages)
  • FlowFileDocument.deleteContentPages()

  • partPages = ContentAction.getContent()
  • ContentAction.setContent(partPages)

  • ContentAction.readContent(...)
  • ContentAction.transformContent(...)
  • ContentAction.writeContent(...)
  • with ContentAction.readContent() as inputstream
  • with ContentAction.transformContent() as (inputstream,outputstream)
  • with ContentAction.writeContent() as outputstream

  • DocumentAction.addContent(...)
  • with DocumentAction.addContent() as outputstream
